The Origins of Cringe

I started this comic on a complete whiim in 2020, when I was in the deepest point of my hyperfixation of the popular webcomic Homestuck, which directly inspired this comic.

It started off as dumb, vibes-based storytelling that eventually grew into a behemoth of a project both narratively and the sheer scale of the worldbuilding.

About the Comic

Awakening Rift adopts the same premise as it's source of inspiration: Some kids play a game and inadvertedly end the world.

The perspective shifts between a primary cast of twelve aliens (Trolls), who grapple with the fate of their homeworld, the purpose of the game they play, and worst of all, interpersonal issues... Oh, and the eldritch horrors the cosmos brings.
